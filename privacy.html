Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Mirah (“we”, “us”, the “App”) is an AI-powered digital closet and outfit planner for Muslim and modest-fashion women, for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what we collect, why, how it is stored, and the choices you have. We collect only what the App needs to work. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

This App is operated by Made It Happen LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company, United States. Questions: info@mirah.support. Most of Mirah works the same on iPhone and Android; where it doesn’t, this policy says which. Where it says iPhone, or your phone, that includes the same app on iPad.

How your data is used by AI

Some Mirah features use AI services run by other companies: Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude). The first time you use one, Mirah shows you what will be sent and who receives it, and asks your permission. If you say no, nothing is sent, and your closet, the outfits you put together yourself and wear tracking keep working. Data goes from the App to our secure backend, which sends it to the provider and returns the result to you.

  • Adding clothes (Google): each photo you add, so Gemini can find the garments in it (a mirror or outfit photo includes you), then a close-up of each piece you keep, to read its category, colours, coverage and formality.
  • Studio (Google): the cut-out of the piece you choose, to create a cleaner photo of it.
  • Colour analysis, optional (Google): one photo of your face, to read your skin undertone. If you pick your undertone by hand, no photo is sent.
  • Outfit suggestions (Anthropic): no photos. A text description of your pieces, the occasion, your style answers (including hijab preference and cultural background) and hijab shades, what you thought of earlier looks, any note you type, and, with weather-aware styling, the current temperature and conditions. Sent when you ask for an outfit and, once a day, for your daily pick, which also uses what you’ve worn lately.

Your hijab preference and cultural background can reveal your religion or ethnic origin. They are sent only for outfit suggestions, and only after you agree. Your name, email address, location and payment details are never sent to either provider. Your photos are never made public; closet images live in a private, per-user storage bucket accessible only with your authenticated session.

To avoid repeat work, our server may keep Gemini’s answer about a photo you add (the garments it found and what it read about each piece, not the photo itself) for up to 7 days, tied to your account, so adding the same photo again doesn’t send it to Google again.

Who we share data with

We share your data only with the service providers below, only to do the job listed, and we never sell it. Each is bound by its contract or terms with us to give your data the same or equal protection as this policy: to use it only to provide its service to Mirah and to keep it secure and confidential.

  • Google (Gemini API) — reads your clothing photos and, if you choose, your face photo, and creates Studio photos. Under Google’s terms for paid services, it does not use them to improve its products or train its models, and it logs them for a limited time only to detect and prevent misuse.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — writes your outfit suggestions from the text described above. Under Anthropic’s Commercial Terms, it may not train its models on it, and it deletes it within 30 days, keeping it longer only if its automated safety systems flag a violation of its usage policy.
  • Supabase — hosts our database, private photo storage and backend in the United States, and sends account emails, such as a password-reset link, to your email address, directly or through an email-delivery provider.
  • Apple — on iPhone, handles payments and Sign in with Apple. Apple WeatherKit returns the current weather for weather-aware styling in both apps: on iPhone it receives your location from your phone; on Android it receives the rounded location from our server, never from your phone, with nothing that identifies you.
  • Google Play (Android) — handles payments in the Android app. When we switch on Google Play Integrity, Google checks that requests come from the genuine Mirah app on a genuine device, using a scrambled code made from your account ID (never the ID itself), and our server receives Google’s verdict. We use it only to stop abuse.
  • Google Firebase (Android) — Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring receive the usage events, crash reports and performance measurements described above, with the scrambled code and identifiers for the App’s installation, never your account ID.
  • Google ML Kit (Android) — runs on your phone to make cut-outs and to frame your face photo, and doesn’t send your photos anywhere. Google receives basic diagnostics from it (phone model and system version, app version, how it performed) with an identifier for the App’s installation, and says it does not pass these to anyone else.
  • Google Sign-In — confirms who you are if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • RevenueCat — tracks your subscription and Enhance credits in both apps using your account ID. On iPhone, its software also sends the device identifier described above; on Android, we have turned that off.
  • TelemetryDeck (iPhone) — receives the usage events described above, carrying the scrambled code, never your account ID.

We don’t share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How it is stored and protected

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase (Postgres database and private file storage). Every user-owned record is protected by row-level security so one account can never read another’s data. Payments are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android; subscription state is mirrored via RevenueCat. We retain your data for as long as your account exists, with these exceptions: Studio’s copies last 30 days at most, saved AI answers about your photos 7 days at most, and, on Android, the weather for an area 30 minutes at most. Usage events and crash reports are kept by TelemetryDeck and Google Firebase until they expire: in Firebase Analytics, after the retention period we set there; otherwise, on each service’s own schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sending data to AI providers any time from Profile → Privacy → AI styling → Withdraw. Once you withdraw, nothing more is sent until you agree again; Mirah asks the next time you use a feature that needs it, and you can say no. While sharing is off, Profile → Privacy → AI styling → Review shows you what is sent and lets you agree. Withdrawing doesn’t remove your closet; to remove that, delete pieces or your account. Your choice is saved on each device, so withdraw on each device you use.
  • Edit or delete individual items any time from your closet.
  • Delete your account in the App from Profile → Delete Account, on the web at mirah.support/delete-account.html, or by emailing us. This permanently and irreversibly erases your profile, profile photo, closet, photos (Studio photos and their copies included), outfit history, saved AI answers, reports and remaining credits from our database and photo storage. It does not cancel a Mirah+ subscription: cancel it first with Apple or Google Play, or it keeps renewing. Usage events and crash reports held by TelemetryDeck and Google Firebase, and the purchase records Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep, are not deleted with your account; email us if you want the RevenueCat record removed too. We keep a record of your account ID and when the account was deleted, to make sure nothing is left behind. Emails you sent us stay in our mailbox, routine server logs and backups kept by Supabase expire on their own schedule, and the AI providers keep requests only as described above. A guest account (no email or sign-in) can be deleted only in the App.
  • Turn off weather/location by declining the location permission or not using weather-aware styling — the App still works.
  • Access or export your data, or ask questions, by emailing info@mirah.support.

If you are in a region with specific privacy rights (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA), you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your data, and you may lodge a complaint with your local authority. We honor these requests at info@mirah.support.

Children

Mirah is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, you may use Mirah only with the permission of a parent or guardian, as our Terms of Use say. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email info@mirah.support and we will delete it.
